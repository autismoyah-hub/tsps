## The set

Furniture for the **observatory of an astronomers' tower**: the working room at the top, where the wardens who carry
the Meridian arms keep their instruments. It is a sister set to the scribes' hall (same hand, same level of detail,
same posts-and-plinth joinery where there is joinery) in a different palette and with instruments instead of books.

- **Read for the house style:** the scribes' desk (`{ROOT}/builds/original-scribe-desk/`: its
  `design.md` and `notes.md` for how tones are placed, post and plinth sizes, how props are sized) and the Meridian
  staff and glaive (`{ROOT}/builds/meridian-staff/design.md`, `meridian-glaive/design.md`) for the
  observatory's materials and motifs. Draw them in your line-ups: the desk as an object entry; the staff is a weapon
  and is only for reading.
- **Materials and palette.** Dark walnut for all woodwork (hue 5 to 6, saturation 3 to 4, lightness about 16 to 40 in
  four or five tones; darker and cooler than the scribes' golden wood); warm brass for instruments and fittings (hue 7
  to 8, saturation 4 to 5, lightness about 45 to 70 in three or four tones); deep blue for enamel, cloth and painted
  sky (hue 37 to 38, saturation 4 to 5, lightness about 20 to 40); the house steel for small working parts only (hue
  42, saturation 0, lightness about 35 to 68); parchment for charts (the desk's paper colours). Mostly walnut; brass is
  the second material; blue is one field per piece.
- **Motifs, from astronomers' instruments:** a ring or arc, a small sphere held in a ring, a four- or eight-point
  star, a crescent, graduated notches along an edge. One motif per piece, large enough to read at game size.
- **Lighting values** for every line-up: `--ambient 30 --contrast 0`.
- **Judge beside:** the scribes' desk (same hand?), the other finished observatory pieces if your task line names any,
  and real objects of your kind.

Your piece, its size range, face range and stated direction are in the task line that came with this brief.
