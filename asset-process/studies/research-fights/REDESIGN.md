**The conflict that gives the fight its structure (R11).** In Tekton's fight, flinching out of reach is the answer to his swing, but staying out of reach sends him to the anvil. The Warden's equivalent is the dial.

- The dial is the only shelter from the Sighting.
- The dial must also be arrested before it feeds him.
- Arrest it before the volley (absolute tick 19) and the volley hits for 60.
- Arrest it after tick 22 and he heals 45.
- The answer is a learned order: shelter, take the volley on the dial, then arrest.
- In phase 2 the same order applies to two dials inside 5 ticks.

**Rotation and timeline.** The order is fixed and can be learned (R10, 0.78 fixed). Phase 1 runs on a 5-tick beat, 30 ticks per rotation, about 20 attacks a minute:

1. Lance
2. Orbit
3. Lance
4. Sighting
5. Lance
6. Line

Phase 2 uses the same order on a 4-tick beat, with the pair of dials and the cross. Phase 3 is phase 2 with double Lances.

- **Cadence:** fightcheck measures 20.9 attacks a minute and an input share of 0.16 over the timeline. Both are inside the real bands (R15 13.3-30; R16 0.10-0.40), and both rise with each phase.
- **Lethality:** no single failure is lethal. Two consecutive failures can be, for example Cross (44) and an unprayed Double Lance (64) in phase 3. That places him with the bosses at a 65% worst single failure (R7 median 79).

**Not added, and why.**

- **No flinch melee.** Only 4 of 154 measured mechanics use a flinch, so the rules do not ask for one. Copying Tekton's flinch would make him a carbon copy, which the owner did not want.
- **No second prayer style.** No rule requires one. A random style switch would also lower the learnable share.

The server work is:

- The heal when a dial is released, and the 50% and 20% triggers.
- Orbit still borrows Tekton's 7484 for its pose. It is the only attack without one of his own sequences.

## How the review films must show the structure

The owner said he did not know the mechanics, so each mechanic needs a **pair of clips** played side by side on one clock (`fight.py film` already does this): a player who solves it and a player who fails and is punished.

Two harness additions are needed first:

1. **The player must be able to move.** fight.py playback currently [quoted words withheld]. It needs player walk steps, which tools-real-fight already added for the NPC.
