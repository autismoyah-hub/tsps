# Build 951 and OSRS 239 study

RS3 columns contain direct model IDs from the definition. Faces, vertices, materials, textures, transparency and bones per vertex are unavailable for every RS3 row because archive 47 contains zero resident groups. OSRS columns measure the decoded inventory or body meshes before welding. Texture count is distinct assigned texture IDs after available item swaps. Alpha counts faces with a nonzero transparency byte. Bones is the maximum number of explicit nonzero influences on any vertex, excluding classic labels.

Controls match encounter role, body plan, weapon type or armour slot. The sheets show the OSRS controls only, with an independently framed static triangle preview. Shading uses face colour and alpha without texture sampling, animation, definition scaling or game lighting. The creature selections identify modern encounters by name. Release dates are not recorded in the definitions.

## Mesh numbers from the flat-file export (added after the models arrived)

The OpenRS2 flat-file export contains all 146,939 archive-47 groups. The numbers below come from `rs3model.py` through `study_mesh.py`, with the same definitions and the same OSRS controls as above. **RS3 faces** are render-group triangles. **Vertices** are counted two ways. *Stored* is the shared vertex buffer, split wherever a corner differs in normal, UV or colour. *Welded* is the number of distinct positions, the figure comparable to an OSRS vertex count. **Materials** are distinct non-empty group materials after the definition retextures. **Textures** are the distinct texture images that those materials reference (diffuse, normal and further slots). **Alpha faces** have at least one corner with vertex alpha below 255. **Max bones** is the largest skin-influence count on any vertex, from vertex flag 0x20. The share in brackets is the fraction of vertices with two or more influences. Models without flag 0x20 have rigid per-vertex labels (flag 0x08) and show 0 here. Raw rows: [study-mesh.json](study-mesh.json). Six-view renders of ten of these: [proof-views](proof-views/contact.png).

Medians: creatures 23,947 RS3 faces against 2,453 for the OSRS controls (median ratio 9.9x). Weapons: 704 against 398 (1.35x). Armour inventory models: 874 against 430 (2.35x). All 33 selections are textured except the shared effect model 197. The median number of textures per creature is 5.5, against 0 assigned textures on every OSRS control.

Observations. The RS3 spot model 197 (spots 91 and 92) is an 8,244-face untextured mesh with 6,982 translucent faces. It is not the small wind-strike projectile, so in build 951 that model ID was reused or rebuilt. Several old-design creatures (Seiryu, Solak, Black stone dragon) carry no skin weights: they are rigged with per-vertex labels only. The modern bosses (Raksha, Kerapac, Vorkath, Croesus, TzKal-Zuk) all use up to four influences per vertex. Vorkath has 61% multi-influence vertices and TzKal-Zuk 56%. OSRS 239 reaches six influences only on the Leviathan control.
