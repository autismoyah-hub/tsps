# Authoring lessons for revision 242

The strongest comparison is the Eldritch crossbow. The ordinary item and its Barrows variant reference the same inventory model and the same worn model. The variant changes five colours and one material. This gives an author a precise, testable rebuild brief even while the original mesh is unavailable. Counts below come from the build-951 definitions and decoded OSRS controls. They describe what the definitions request. Surface coverage, perceived gloss and deformation on 951 remain unmeasured. ## First three blind rebuilds

1. **Eldritch crossbow, item 47470.** Build a fresh inventory silhouette against the 266-face Zaryte control, then a separate hand-fitted model. Give the Barrows finish its own five colour substitutions and one material swap. Success means both variants use identical geometry and remain distinguishable at inventory scale.
2. **Vestments of havoc hood, item 53375.** Build one inventory hood and a fitted assembly. Use four destination colour families and two independently swappable surface regions. The worn-part references let the author test fit separately from the icon. The 280-face control gives a starting budget.
3. **Echo of Kerapac, NPC 28070.** Build an original four-part body and author two appearance definitions. Use six material replacements and two colour replacements for the echo. Verify unchanged body vertex and face buffers between the two authored forms. This exercises the same material technique at encounter scale.

These are provisional choices based on verified structure and the available OSRS controls. A visual ranking of the RS3 silhouettes needs the missing meshes and diffuse images.


## What the meshes add (flat-file export, after the models arrived)

The 951 meshes now decode, so each lesson above can be checked against geometry. Numbers come from [study-mesh.json](study-mesh.json) and [census47.json](census47.json). OSRS figures are the 239 controls.

| Technique | Mesh evidence in 951 | Rebuild in 242 |
|---|---|---|
| One weapon, several finishes | The crossbow inventory model 74787 has 943 triangles over 514 welded positions (1,065 stored vertices) in three render groups. It uses two materials (10938, 10942). The Barrows variant swaps only 10942 to 10943. Every vertex is bound to a single bone. The OSRS Zaryte control has 266 faces. | **Yes.** Author one mesh and give it two definitions. The finish lives in the material and paint, not in the geometry. Budget about 500 to 950 faces, or keep the 266-face OSRS density if you want the shape at OSRS fidelity. |
| Fit, palette and materials separated | The hood inventory model has 524 faces and 2 materials, with 1 bone per vertex. The male worn form is two models with 4,052 faces, 3 materials and up to 4 influences per vertex (41% of vertices multi-bone). The inventory and worn meshes differ completely. | **Yes** for separate meshes. Multi-influence fitting **needs our weighted (skinned) layout**: build the worn parts with explicit byte weights on the target rig. |
| Encounter state over a shared body | Kerapac and the echo share 4 body models: 18,788 faces and 9 materials, with 35% multi-influence vertices. The echo's substitutions change material and paint only. Vertex and face buffers are byte-identical between the two appearances, because the same groups are referenced. | **With our texture-and-transparency support.** The body must be rebuilt at a 242 budget: the original exceeds one 242 model's 16-bit index stream. |
| Many images per material | 20,724 diffuse textures are referenced first by a material. Slot statistics confirm the second slot is a tangent-space normal map: mean (2 to 35, 128, 247 to 255) in the PNG archive. Every textured study model uses 2 to 24 texture images. | A single-image approximation in 242. Normal and other maps **need a client change**. |
| Vertex alpha instead of face alpha | Alpha is per vertex in RS3 and interpolated across the face. Render-group flag 0x02 marks exactly the groups with any vertex alpha below 255 (consistent in all 146,939 models). | Converted to face transparency as 255 minus the mean corner alpha. Smooth fades need finer triangulation in 242. |
| Skinning density | 34,414 of 146,939 models carry skin weights (flag 0x20). Of those, 18,686 have vertices with 2 to 4 influences. The maximum anywhere is 4, and weights are bytes that sum to 256. | **Yes** within our weighted layout's limits, which already handle 6 on the Leviathan control. |
