# What a boss arena is, in numbers (measured; a hypothesis until the owner judges an arena built to it)

Status 2026-11-27. Private to this machine (cache-derived). Measured from 26 real end-game boss arenas located in the
revision-239 map, each drawn with its boss by the client's own map loader and painter and looked at. Folder:
`{ROOT}/studies/research-arenas/`. `P` = `{PYTHON} -I`, run there.

```
P probe.py plan X Y PLANE [R] | find REGEX [box] | ids ID,..     look at a piece of the map before calling it an arena
P draw.py [KEY ..]            the real arenas with their bosses -> render/research-arenas/KEY-{top,top-empty,played,threeq,check}.png
P measure.py [KEY ..]         every measure -> data/arenas.json          P table.py  -> data/bands.json, data/tables.md
P rules.py                    every rule with the number of real arenas it holds for -> data/rules.txt
P arenacheck.py PLACEMENTS.csv FLOOR.json      one of ours beside the real band (a guard, not a judge)
P arenacheck.py --real KEY                     the same report for a real arena
P compose_arena.py TAG [--floor dark|boards|flags]     the Warden-General's first arena from existing pieces
P contact.py boards ; P assemble.py            the boards; this file from ARENAS.src.md + the generated tables
```

**What the checker is and is not.** `arenacheck.py` prints each measure of an arena beside the band of the 26 real
ones and each rule below as `holds` / `OUTSIDE` / `n/a`. It never prints PASS. The real arenas themselves hold a
median 21 of the 25 rules (fewest: Bloat 16 of 24; only the Maiden's holds all 25, and Muspah's all 24 that can be
measured on it): an arena inside every band is not
unusual, no more. It has not been calibrated against any grade of the owner's: no arena of ours has been judged.

## How the arenas were found and drawn

- **Where.** By the owner's server copy (`npc-spawns.json`, `plugins/bosses/**`: Duke, Vardorvis, Whisperer, Hydra
  spawns; Nex, Nightmare, Maggot King, Doom, Araxxor plugin coordinates), by a named object of the fight found in the
  map (`probe.py find`: Tekton's Giant Anvil 29867, the Olm's Mystical barrier 29879, Xarpus' Barrier 32755, the
  Tombs' teleport crystals), or by coordinates I know (marked `known`). Every one was then drawn with its boss and
  looked at: `boards/board-real-check-1..3.jpg` (the measured floor in yellow, the boss's tiles in cyan).
- **The floor** of an arena is every tile a player can walk to from a tile inside it, by the client's own collision
  rules (`arenalib.World.floor`: terrain flag 1, wall objects on tile edges, standing objects that block). Corridors and
  antechambers were cut off by a clip box in `arenas.py` where a barrier does not block in the map (Nex, Doom, Maiden,
  Sotetseg, Whisperer, Vardorvis).
- **The pictures** are the client's own: `client/rust-client/examples/arena_view.rs` = the effects harness
  `asset_arena.rs` plus one script line `plane N` (generated by `rust/patch_arena_view.py`; asset_arena drew plane 0
  only and the Nightmare stands on plane 3, Tekton on plane 1). Real terrain, real textures, the boss as an NPC through
  the client's actor path. The camera takes the same six numbers and the same lens as `tools/scene.py`'s capture, so a
  real arena and one of ours are framed alike. Three cameras: `top` (straight down, the floor and the boss in frame),
  `threeq` (from the south-west), `played` (behind the player's tile, 2300 back, 1650 up: a zoomed-out game look).
- **The picture measures** (`palette.py`) are taken on the straight-down view: the boss is every pixel that differs
  between the picture with him and the picture without; the floor is the measured floor tiles projected into the
  picture (`draw.project`, checked by the yellow outlines).

## The words

- **Floor**: tiles a player can walk on, reached from inside. **Outline**: the floor with every enclosed hole filled
  (pits, pillars, a blocked centre). **Outline edge**: one tile-length of the outline's border.
- **Short side / box**: of the outline's bounding box. **Boss tiles**: his size squared (a 4-tile boss covers 16).
- **Centre zone**: within 0.4 of the inradius of the outline's middle. **Band**: the outermost two rings of tiles.
- **Floor kind**: the ground painted on a tile (an underlay or an overlay of the map; for ours, the floor piece's kind).
  **Floor decoration**: an object lying on the floor (the game's shape 22: pattern tiles, grates, inlays, stains).
- **Edge objects**: everything modelled within two tiles outside the outline, and wall pieces on the outline's own
  tiles; the game's invisible wall objects (`inviswall*`, `blankwall`, `*blockrange`, `greybox*`) are not counted.
  **Beyond**: three to six tiles outside. **Inside**: standing objects on tiles of the outline.
- **Bounded by**: for each outline edge, what stops the player: a `wall` piece on the edge, an `object` on the next
  tile (rock, pillar, fence, furniture), `void` (no ground there: a drop into dark), `other ground` (water, lava,
  a different ground that is blocked), `set back` (nothing on the edge itself, something modelled one or two tiles
  behind it: the Theatre's rooms put an invisible wall a step inside their real walls), `unseen` (nothing), `clip`
  (the map does not block there at all: my clip box).
- **delta E**: CIE76 colour distance between two mean colours (2 is barely visible, 10 clear, 30 strong).
  **Brightness**: 0..255 (0.299 R + 0.587 G + 0.114 B). Units: 128 = one tile.

## The rules

Each rule: what it says, how many of the 26 real arenas it holds for (the table below is generated by `rules.py`), and
what to build to. Limits written `p10` / `p90` are the tenth and ninetieth centile of the real arenas; "aim" is their
middle half (p25 .. p75).

<<RULECOUNTS>>

### Shape and size

**A1. Symmetric outline** (24 of 26). The outline mirrors about its middle line: mirror share 1.00 at the median, 0.90
or more in 24. The two that do not are a fungus clearing (Maggot King) and a cave room (Tekton).
Outlines found: round or octagonal 9, square 9, rectangle 3, a box with cut corners 1, irregular 4. No cross, no L.

**A2. Compact** (21 of 26). The outline fills 0.85 or more of its convex hull and is at most 1.45 times as long as wide
(median 1.08). The long ones are processions to a boss at the far end: Sotetseg 14 x 26, the Olm 10 x 19.

**A3. Size** (band by construction). Short side 13 to 27 tiles, aim 16 to 24 (median 19.5); floor 199 to 640 tiles,
aim 250 to 560 (median 335). Smallest Nylocas 12 x 12 (128 tiles), largest the Inferno 29 x 30 and the Colosseum
33 x 34 (870 each).

**A4. Room for the boss** (20 of 26). The short side is at least 2.8 bosses (aim 3.0 to 4.7, median 3.7); the floor is
7 to 34 tiles per boss tile (aim 9 to 21, median 13). Arena size does NOT follow the boss's size (correlation of boss
side with floor area 0.26, with the short side 0.16): it follows the fight. For a 4-tile boss (16 tiles): short side 12
to 19, floor 150 to 340 tiles; the two real 4-tile bosses have 12 x 12 / 128 (Nylocas) and 19 x 23 / 237 (Tekton).

**A5. A clear fighting floor** (23 of 26). The largest clear square of floor is at least 1.4 bosses a side (aim 2.0 to
3.4, median 2.8): for a 4-tile boss a clear 8 x 8 at the least, 11 x 11 typically.

**A6. The boss stands on the middle line** (24 of 26): in the centre (15 arenas), off centre on the axis (4: Maiden at
one end of her room, Ba-Ba, Tekton by his anvil, Vardorvis), at the edge (4: Sotetseg, Zebak, Bloat's ring, Maggot King)
or beyond the edge altogether, outside the floor (3: Zuk behind the north edge, the Duke in his alcove, the Olm in the
wall). Never in a corner. A boss in the centre has a median 4 floor tiles between him and the outline.

**A7. Players come in at the edge, away from the boss** (17 of the 18 where an entry object was found). An entry is an
object ON the edge with an option: a barrier to Pass (the Theatre's rooms, Nex, the Nightmare, the Olm), a gap, a
passage, a door, steps. One entry in 11 arenas, two in 6 (in and out, opposite), four in 1. Distance from the entry to
the boss's nearest tile: 5 to 12 tiles in the middle half (median 8.5); the least is 2 (Bloat).

### Floor

**F1. Few kinds of ground** (23 of 26). At most four kinds make 95% of the floor (median 3). One kind covers a median
0.77 of it (0.50 to 0.97 in the middle half).

**F2. Flat** (23 of 26; 17 are perfectly flat). Four tiles in five are dead flat. The exceptions are deliberate ground:
Zebak's sand island (0 flat), Ba-Ba's ramp (0.22), the Maggot King's clearing (0.23). Steps and daises are rare: only 9
arenas have any height difference on the floor at all.

**F3. One field, few colours** (22 of 26). In the picture a median 0.79 of the floor lies within delta E 10 of the
floor's own median colour (at least 0.44), and 4 colours make 90% of it (at most 8). The busy floors: Ba-Ba (0.33, 13 colours), the
Inferno's glow (0.35, 10 colours), Akkha's dial (0.44), Kephri's mosaic (12 colours).

**F4. Where the floor is marked, the marking is symmetric** (17 of the 19 with 5% or more of the floor marked; mirror
share median 0.96). How floors are marked, counted: by ground kind the centre zone differs from the middle in 10 of 26
and the border ring in 10 of 26; in the picture the centre differs visibly (delta E 6 or more) in 8 (the Nightmare's
inlay, Doom's ring, Verzik's lane, Kephri's mosaic, Ba-Ba, the Inferno's glow, the Colosseum's sand disc, Vorkath's
dais) and the band in 11. Concentric marking (rings about the middle) is the common form: 16 of 26 change their main
ground kind at least once going outward. **Most of the pattern is objects, not ground**: floor decoration objects lie on
a median 0.61 of the floor (0.14 or more in 19 of 26; a median 8.5 distinct pieces), where a furnished room has 0.31.

**F5. The floor is not vivid** (23 of 26). Floor saturation at most 0.53 (median 0.33). Floors are DARK: brightness
median 51 of 255; 16 of 26 floors are under 60. The light floors are snow, ice and sand (Nex 153, Vorkath 156, Muspah
145, Vardorvis 136, Ba-Ba 110, the Colosseum 103).

### Edge

**E1. An edge you can see** (24 of 26). 0.80 or more of the outline is bounded by something seen. What bounds an arena,
by its main cause: standing objects (rock, cave wall, pillars, trees, rails) 17; a wall or object set one or two tiles
back behind an invisible wall 3 (Maiden, Xarpus, Duke); a drop into dark 3 (Akkha's and the Wardens' platforms, the
Inferno); wall pieces on the edge 1 (Kephri); water 1 (Zebak); a low wall the map does not block 1 (Vardorvis).

**E2. The edge is dressed thickly** (23 of 26). At least 2.2 modelled objects within two tiles of the outline per 10
outline edges; aim 4.6 to 13 (median 6.9). A real arena has a median 14.5 kinds of edge piece.

**E3. Few kinds, repeated** (21 of 26). 6.5 kinds make 80% of the edge at the median (at most 15), each kind used
4.6 times on average (at least 2.0); the commonest piece is a third of the edge (0.34).

**E4. Tall pieces stand in the edge** (23 of 26). Standing pieces 200 units or taller: at least 0.55 per 10 outline
edges, aim 0.8 to 5.2 (median 2.1: one every five tiles). The edge's tall things: 90th centile height at least 130,
median 487 (3.8 tiles); the tallest thing at the edge is 500 units or more in 19 of 26 (median 572). The edge's MEDIAN
piece is 328 units, but 7 arenas keep their edge low (under 140: the Nightmare's rail 135, Sotetseg 120, Xarpus 107, Vorkath 87,
Bloat 67, the Inferno 63, Zebak 55), so that the camera sees over it from every side.

**E5. Almost nothing is hung on the walls** (23 of 26 hang nothing at all; the rule allows 0.5 per 10 edges). The
exceptions: Doom, Kephri, Ba-Ba (torches and brackets). The game models wall detail INTO its wall pieces and stands
things in front of them; ROOMS.md's wall-decoration rule (D5) does not carry over.

**E6. There is a world beyond the edge** (24 of 26 by the measure, 26 of 26 by eye). Three to six tiles outside the outline there is ground, or at
least 4 objects per 10 outline edges (median 11): the cave goes on, the building's lower roofs show (the Nightmare),
the platform has its underside and neighbours (Akkha, the Wardens), lava and rock (the Inferno, Tekton), forest
(Maggot King). The two the measure misses (the Nightmare, Nylocas) stand over structure on LOWER planes, which the
measure does not read; in the pictures both have their building under and round them. No real arena is a floor in
nothing.

### Inside

**I1. The floor is open** (24 of 26). 0.90 or more of the outline's inside is walkable (median 1.00) and objects block
at most 0.05 of it (0 in 21 of 26). Exceptions: Nex's four pits (0.34 of the inside is pit: the floor IS a cross in a
ring), Bloat's central block.

**I2. Few things stand inside** (23 of 26): at most 3.3 per 100 tiles; 14 of 26 arenas have NOTHING standing inside the
outline. What does stand inside is the fight's own: the Colosseum's 4 pillars, Vardorvis' pillars, the Wardens'
obelisk, the Hydra's 3 vents, or litter at the edge (bones, rubble, drips: Araxxor 16, Duke 12).

**I3. The centre is empty** (24 of 26; the exceptions are Bloat's block and the Wardens' obelisk, both the fight's
subject). Light sources by name are too rare to rule on (3 of 26 name one); animated objects are common at the edge
(1 or more in 17 of 26, median 1.5; the Whisperer 25, the Nightmare 14, Tekton 10) and rare inside (4 of 26).

### Palette and focus

**P1. The boss is off the floor's colour** (19 of 26). delta E between the boss and the floor under him at least 15
(median 20, middle half 15 to 31). Not: Maggot King 13, Zebak 13, Ba-Ba 15 (a hair under), Hydra 14, the Duke asleep 14,
Verzik's last form 10, Xarpus dormant 7.

**P2. The boss is off the floor's brightness** (21 of 26). At least 13 of 255 apart (median 25, middle half 16 to 51).
On a light floor (80 or brighter) the boss is the darker of the two in 9 of 10; on a dark floor he is the brighter in 9
of 16. 23 of 26 hold P1 or P2; the three that hold neither (Maggot King, Ba-Ba, Zebak) are bosses of the floor's own hue.

**P3. The edge is not brighter than the floor** (23 of 26). The two tiles round the outline are darker than the floor
in 19 of 26 (median 9.5 darker; at most 6.7 brighter): the floor is the lit stage, the edge frames it.

**P4. The vivid colour sits at the edge, not on the floor** (19 of 26). Of the most vivid 2% of the picture
(saturation times value), a median 0.95 lies outside the floor: lava, crystals, braziers, banners, glowing barriers.
The 7 exceptions carry a vivid motif ON the floor (the Nightmare's green lanes, Verzik's purple lane, Kephri's mosaic,
Bloat, Ba-Ba, the Wardens, Vorkath's dais), and in each it is the marking of F4, symmetric about the boss.

**Where the eye is led** (reported, not ruled): to the boss by brightness or colour against a dark, plain, flat floor;
to the far side on the door's line when the boss stands there (throne, anvil, altar, alcove); never to the middle of a
side wall. The floor's centre is brighter than its band by a median 3.5 only: light is not what marks the middle,
pattern is.

## What an arena is not: ROOMS.md's rules run on the arenas

`measure.py` ran `roomcheck.check` (kind `any`) on every arena as if it were a furnished room, twice: counting only
what the game names as furniture (as the rooms study did), and counting every standing object. Arenas passing each
room rule (named / every object), of 26:

| room rule | named | every object | verdict for an arena |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 pieces | 1 | 3 | **do not apply**: arenas hold 0.56 standing objects per 10 tiles (rooms 1.81), all at the edge |
| D2 floor used | 1 | 5 | **do not apply**: objects cover 0.11 of an arena (rooms 0.28) |
| D3 walls lined | 7 | 20 | carries over in spirit: 0.71 of the edge has something against it (rooms 0.42); use E2 |
| D4 no bare wall | 6 | 13 | **do not apply**: longest bare run 4.5 tiles at the median, 9 at p75 (rooms 3 and 4) |
| D5 wall decoration | 0 | 0 | **do not apply**: no arena reaches it; see E5 |
| D6 variety | 5 | 20 | use E3 instead |
| D7 corners | 11 | 22 | carries over: corners are dressed (often cut off: octagons) |
| P1 against walls | 20 | 25 | carries over: 1.00 of what stands, stands at the edge |
| P2 wall pieces at walls | 24 | 23 | trivially met |
| P3 groups, P4 companions | 9 / 24 | 19 / 25 | **do not apply**: there are no seats, tables or beds to pair |
| P5 repetition | 13 | 21 | carries over, stronger: the commonest edge piece repeats 6 times at the median (16 at p75) |
| P6 faces the door | 9 | 14 | weak: use A6 (the boss is what faces the door) |
| P7 centre clear | 26 | 25 | carries over as I3 |
| P8 reach | 3 | 3 | not measurable (few arenas have a door the room tool can read) |
| P9 free floor | 2 | 9 | **do not apply as written**: its upper limit (0.87) fails arenas, which are freer (0.90 median, 0.95 at p75) |

So: **never run roomcheck on an arena.** Its density rules (D1, D2, D4, D5), its pairing rules (P3, P4) and the upper
limit of P9 would all ask for furniture on a fighting floor. What carries over is placement at the edge, repetition and
the clear centre, and here they are stronger than in rooms.

## How to build an arena for a boss of S tiles (the order of work)

1. Decide where the boss stands (centre; an end on the axis; or beyond the far edge) and where players enter (the edge,
   on the same axis, 5 to 12 tiles from him).
2. Outline: round, octagonal or square, symmetric, short side 3 to 4.7 x S and inside 16 to 24 tiles unless the fight
   says otherwise; a clear square of at least 2 x S, typically 2.8 x S.
3. Floor: one dark, unsaturated field (brightness 39 to 90, saturation under 0.42) at least 15 delta E and 16
   brightness off the boss; flat; at most 4 kinds; a symmetric marking about the boss (a ring, a centre panel, lanes to
   the edge), made of floor decoration pieces.
4. Edge: bounded all round by something seen; 5 to 13 pieces per 10 outline edges from 4 to 10 kinds, the commonest a
   third of them; a tall piece (200 units or more) every 2 to 12 tiles; the tallest 480 to 770; low (under 140) where
   the camera must see over it; nothing hung; the vivid colour and anything animated here.
5. Beyond: ground or objects three to six tiles out (6 to 23 objects per 10 outline edges).
6. Inside: nothing, unless the fight uses it; then few (under 3.3 per 100 tiles), symmetric, not in the centre.
7. Run `arenacheck.py`. Explain every OUTSIDE line or fix it.

## The Warden-General's arena, worked (`compose_arena.py r1 --floor dark`)

The top of the astronomers' tower from pieces that already exist; nothing was authored. A 17 x 17 court with the
observatory kit's splayed corners (floor 263 tiles after the edge pieces take theirs; 16.4 tiles per boss tile; short
side 4.25 bosses; clear square 13 = 3.25 bosses). Floor: the observatory's boards, their border course along the walls,
its 5 x 5 star dial in the middle under the boss (3 kinds, symmetric). Edge: the dark observatory masonry (304 high)
with windows at regular bays, the door in the south wall (the entry, 7 tiles from the boss), and standing against the
walls two suits of armour a side as the statues (8, 207 high), a brazier in each corner (4), two telescopes on the far
wall; hung, the rule's limit of four: two standards on the far wall on the door's line behind the boss, a shield on each
side wall. Nothing stands on the fighting floor. No ground outside: the court stands in the dark.

Pictures: `boards/board-warden-arena-r1.jpg` (ours above Tekton's room and the Nightmare's arena, the same three
cameras), `boards/board-warden-floors.jpg`, `render/research-arenas-warden-r1/`. The compose table and floor
description: `warden-arena-r1.placements.csv`, `warden-arena-r1.floor.json`; the report: `warden-arena-r1.check.txt`.

**arenacheck: 22 of 25 rules hold. OUTSIDE:**
- **F5 floor not vivid**: floor saturation 0.56 (real at most 0.53, median 0.33). The observatory's boards are the most
  saturated thing in the picture.
- **P4 vivid colour at the edge**: 0.05 of the vivid pixels are outside the floor (real median 0.95): same cause.
- **E6 a world beyond the edge**: nothing stands outside the walls (real median 11 objects per 10 outline edges).
Also flagged low or high beside the band without a rule: no floor decoration objects (real median 0.61 of the floor),
no animated edge object (real median 1.5), the edge's tallest thing 304 (real median 572), the boss's own saturation
0.12 (real bosses 0.27 to 0.55).

**The same court on the armoury's grey flags** (`--floor flags`, `warden-arena-r1-flags.check.txt`): F5 and P4 hold, but
**P1 and P2 fail hard: the boss is 2.9 delta E and 7 brightness off the floor** (real at least 15 and 13). A giant in
grey plate on grey flags is the floor's own colour. Of the two floors that exist, the boards are the one he can be seen
on (delta E 27.8, brightness 13.9: P2 only just), which is why r1 uses them; neither is an arena floor.

**Honest notes on the pictures.** Our boss is his model (`builds/boss-warden-general-3`) placed as a 4 x 4 static
object in its rest pose and lit as scenery by the room renderer, the way `tools/scene.py` stands a figure in a room;
the real bosses are NPCs drawn by the client's actor path. P1 and P2 for ours are therefore measured under object
light. The south wall is cut away for the played and three-quarter cameras (the project's room practice); the real
arenas are drawn whole. A 4-tile boss cannot stand centred on a 5 x 5 dial in a 17-wide court: he is half a tile off it.

## Limits of these rules, and what I am unsure of

- **The floor is what the MAP blocks.** Fights are instanced and the server may block more or less: Vardorvis' low wall
  does not block in the map (I clipped his floor to it by eye; his edge reads `clip`), the Maggot King's floor is a
  jagged clearing between fungus trees and may not be the whole fight floor, the Duke's includes his two preparation
  wings. Objects the server spawns in a fight (Verzik's pillars, Nex's hazards, the Olm's crystals) are not in the map:
  "inside" counts are lower bounds. The Olm's head and hands were added for the pictures only.
- **Boss positions** marked `known` in `arenas.py` are from my knowledge of the fights, not from the map, and each
  boss was drawn in ONE form (Xarpus dormant, the Duke asleep, Verzik's last form, the Maiden full): P1 and P2 are
  for that form on that tile.
- **`void` merges two things**: a true drop into dark, and a surround drawn by objects that neither block nor stand on
  ground (the Inferno's rock rim). `set back` and the invisible-wall names are a name rule on the game's symbols.
- **Lights** cannot be told from names (3 of 26 name one); "animated" is used instead and counts anything with a
  sequence (smoke, drips, banners), not only fire.
- **Palette** is measured straight down, in the client's software painter at brightness 0.8, with no fight effects. The
  boss seen from above shows his head and shoulders; from the played camera his front. Colour bins are coarse (512).
- **Bands are p10 .. p90 of 26**: by construction about 21 of 26 hold a pure band rule (A3, parts of A4, E2, E3); only
  the rules with a stated number (A1, A2, A6, A7, F1, F2, F4, E1, E5, E6, I1, I3, P1, P2, P4) say something a count
  can support. Raid rooms are 13 of the 26: the set leans towards built rooms.
- **Height** is read per tile; a stepped dais one tile wide counts as not flat. Tile heights with no stored value on
  plane 0 use my port of the client's noise function (not checked against the client pixel for pixel; the yellow
  outlines on Vardorvis and Vorkath fit).
- **The checker reads a compose table**: what it calls floor is every shape-22 piece not named a cut tile, and a
  standing piece against a wall is an EDGE piece (its tile leaves the floor), as a pillar is in the map.
- Nothing here has been seen by the owner. The composed arena is a proof that the rules can be built to and measured
  on our own pieces, not a candidate for a grade: it looks like a large room, and the three OUTSIDE lines and the
  missing pieces say why.
