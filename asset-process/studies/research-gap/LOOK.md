## Warden-General III beside Tekton
1. "Definition" / "crisp": Tekton's body is DARK stone and its marks are BIG bright shapes (lava chevrons, the
   visor slit, the grille on the belly): at game size the marks are the drawing. Ours is mid-grey on mid-grey: plates
   differ from each other by shading alone, and the gold trim is thin lines one or two pixels wide that run parallel to
   every plate edge (a border on every plate). At game size the gold reads as a scatter of short yellow strokes.
2. [quoted words withheld] / "intention": Tekton's planes are big and chiselled; each limb is three or four slabs at different
   angles, so the light breaks hard from plane to plane. Ours is bevelled boxes: every plate has a chamfer ring and
   a soft roll, so shading changes gently everywhere and no plane is decided.
3. "Stuck on": the chest medallion (a dark disc with a star) sits on the cuirass as a sticker with no frame or relief
   seat; the arm blade is a thin bent strip hung off the forearm.
4. Silhouette: Tekton tapers (big shoulders and fists, narrower waist, a tall helm spike); ours is a near-rectangle
   from shoulder to boot with small horn tips: [quoted words withheld] in the sense of no taper and no dominant feature.
