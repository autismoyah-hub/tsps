# Owner update template

For a status report to the owner after a result, a review or a change of plan. Open with the conclusion or the decision needed, in a sentence or two of plain prose. Then the six fields. Guidance is in [core/state-and-handoff.md](../core/state-and-handoff.md).

```markdown
<One or two sentences: what happened and what it means, or what you need from the owner.>

1. Concerns: <the mission or subject>
2. Reference: <exact revision, document or record, with link>
3. Accepted: <what is established, with its grade>
4. Not accepted: <what is open, failed or unknown>
5. Owner action: <the one decision or action needed, with the Teacher's recommendation; or "none">
6. Next: <what proceeds now, who does it, and what does not depend on the owner>
```

Keep it short enough to read on a phone. Detail goes in the repository, linked from field 2.

If there is nothing for the owner to do, say so in field 5; do not invent a question.
