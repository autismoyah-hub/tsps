# Decision record template

Append entries to `project/DECISIONS.md`. Number them in sequence and never reuse a number. A few lines each; link to detail elsewhere. Guidance is in [core/decisions.md](../core/decisions.md).

```markdown
## D-<nnn> <short title>

- Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
- Decided by: owner | Teacher
- Status: open | decided | superseded by D-<nnn> | expired <date> | withdrawn <date>
- Decision: <what was decided, in the decider's terms>
- Reason: <one or two sentences>
- Scope and limits: <for an authorization: what it covers, spend and time limits, when it ends; for a hold: the fact that lifts it and who clears it>
- Teacher's position: <only if it differs: the dissent and what evidence would reopen this>
- Source: <where it was said or recorded>
```

Notes:

- For a question waiting on the owner, use status `open` and write the Decision line as "Recommended: …". These are what a new session reads first.
- When the owner answers an open entry, complete it in place: status `decided`, the Decision line rewritten to what the owner decided, the date updated. Keep your recommendation under "Teacher's position" if it differed.
- When a decided entry is replaced, change only its status and add a new entry. Do not edit a decided entry's substance.
- An authorization that has passed its end time is `expired` whether or not anyone marked it. Mark it when you notice.
- A hold is cleared by whoever owns the record it is written in, and the entry says who that is. Clearing it authorizes nothing that was not authorized before.
- Record owner decisions as the owner stated them. If you are interpreting, say "Teacher's reading:" before the interpretation.
