# Report review template

Use when a mission or any delegated work reports back. Guidance is in [core/missions.md](../core/missions.md) and [core/evidence.md](../core/evidence.md).

```markdown
# Review: <mission or work item>

Verdict: <written last> accepted | accepted in part | not accepted | not established

## Checklist
<Fill every line from the primary record. No verdict until all six are filled;
"not known" is an answer, a blank is not.>
1. Exact revision or artifact the claim covers, and whether it is the one that shipped:
2. Reviewer, their independence from the author, and what they examined:
3. What ran, where, and what did not run:
4. The brief's acceptance condition, word for word, and whether it occurred:
5. Whether every check finished, with its result as read:
6. What the report itself says it did not establish:

## What I examined
<The exact references: commit, document path at revision, logs. What I read
myself and what I am taking from someone's account. What I did not replay.>

## Stage reached
<Highest rung with evidence: proposed, authorized, dispatched, acknowledged,
executed, reviewed, merged, verified in place.>

## The independent review
<Whether the verdict is recorded on the code host or elsewhere.>

## Established
<Claims with grades.>

## Not established
<Line 6 of the checklist, and what the report should have said and did not.>

## Easy misreading
<The one conclusion a hurried reader would draw that the evidence does not support.>

## What this changes
<Effect on the plan and the state. The next action and who takes it.>
```

If the verdict is anything other than accepted, say what specific evidence would move it, so the next step is clear.
