## How acceptance works

This domain has no test suite and no outside maintainer. The owner's judgment of a render is the acceptance.

- The owner: [quoted words withheld]
- Final Blender submissions must show the textured asset (A-006). Clay and placeholder-colour views are construction diagnostics.
- Every picture the owner judges is shown beside the real game asset or assets it should stand next to. A review without the real thing on the page cannot be answered for authenticity (the bench sitting of 2026-11-25 showed this).
- Every asset shown to the owner gets a recorded verdict: pass, fail, or a ranking, with the owner's reason in a line. Verdicts are data; keep them in `docs/verdicts/` and never paraphrase them into something more favourable.
- A reviewer agent may reject an asset. It may not accept one on the owner's behalf until it has earned that for a class of asset, by agreeing with the owner's verdicts on that class over a stated number of cases. Record the agreement rate; it is this adapter's main measure.
- Counts, valid meshes, hashes, checklists and an agent's own review are not acceptance. The earlier work repeatedly declared success on those and the owner then rejected the art.

## Standing rules

Set by the owner unless marked.

1. **Start from the method that worked**, the last lobby study before 2026-11-21, and change it one thing at a time, measuring each change against the owner's verdicts.
2. **Reference first.** A concept reference is made with real same-function game assets attached, then modelled. Authenticity is checked at the reference and at the model.
3. **Objects first, then weapons and armour.** NPCs, animation and the rest come after a method has several passes in a row on objects.
4. **Blender modelling is preferred.** A Hugging Face GPU may be rented [quoted words withheld]. Tell the owner before any paid use, with the expected cost.
5. **Included subscriptions only** for agents: no paid model API, no overage, no fallback provider. Image generation and rented GPU are separate costs under rule 4.
6. **Private material stays private.** Nothing derived from the game's cache or from the owner's private game repositories is published anywhere. Since A-024 the asset system itself (tools, author pack, findings, boards, builds) is kept in this private repository under `asset-system/`; renders, the cache and raw dumps of real meshes are not.
7. **Quality over speed, in the owner's words:** [quoted words withheld] A model finished in minutes has not been looked at. No catalogues or batches from a method that has not passed.
8. **One asset at a time until the method passes**, then small batches. No batch production on an unproven method. (Teacher of the agent-systems project, from the earlier work's record.)
9. **Changes to the role itself** (`teacher/SKILL.md`, `core/`, `templates/`, `check.sh`) are proposed to the repository a private repository, which is their source. Do not let the copy here drift.
